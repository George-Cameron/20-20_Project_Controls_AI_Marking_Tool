How the Marking Companion works

A simple three-step process built around our published course criteria.

1. Choose the module

Select the module the learner’s work is being assessed against (Module A through Module G).

2. Upload the learner’s submission

Attach the learner’s written assignment, report, or exercise workbook as a PDF.

3. Review the mark sheet

The AI returns a criteria-aligned mark sheet with strengths, gaps, and suggested improvements to inform your assessment.
